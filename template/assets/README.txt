Put client photos in this folder, e.g.:

  hero.jpg       - big background image at the top
  about.jpg      - photo for the "About" section
  gallery1.jpg ... gallery6.jpg - gallery images

Then wire them up in styles.css (see ../CUSTOMIZE.md, Step 4).

Tips:
- Use landscape (wide) photos for the hero, ~1600px wide.
- Compress images (e.g. squoosh.app) so the site stays fast.
