# MRLN — Competitor map & positioning (live-researched)

**By:** Maki · **Date:** 2026-07 · **For:** Osefe
**Status:** internal strategy. Re-verify competitors each quarter — this space moves fast.

## Where MRLN sits
Intersection of 3 markets: privacy-first finance · personal finance · health/life tracking.
Competitors come in tiers, not one rival.

## Tier 1 — DIRECT (privacy-first, offline, no-bank-connection finance)
The real neighbors. **This space is now crowded + partly free.**
- **SenticMoney** — local-only, no Plaid, **$39/yr**. ⚠️ MRLN's exact wedge at our exact price. Closest threat.
- **Pocket Clear** — offline, zero server comms, free tier
- **BudgetVault** — 100% local, no account, **free**
- **Monefy** — offline, local, own-Drive/Dropbox sync
- **Finny** — offline-first, 150+ currencies, **$1.99/mo**
- **Actual Budget** — open-source, local-first, **free**
- **Goodbudget** — envelope budgeting, private

## Tier 2 — INCUMBENTS (bank-connected finance) = our FOILS, the villain in our story
- **YNAB** $109/yr · **Copilot** $95/yr · **Monarch** $100/yr · **Rocket Money** · **Simplifi**
- All Plaid-connected + monetize/aggregate data. This is who "we don't want your data" targets.

## Tier 3 — HEALTH (only relevant if we push the health angle)
- **MyFitnessPal, Cronometer, MacroFactor, Lose It, Apple Health.** Entrenched, large data moats.

## The unlisted, biggest competitor
**Free apps + a spreadsheet + apathy.** Most people don't pay for privacy. Bigger wall than any named app.

## TWO HARD TRUTHS (from live 2026 research)
1. **Privacy-first offline finance is no longer a moat — it's a category.** Free options exist (BudgetVault,
   Actual, Pocket Clear) and SenticMoney already runs our exact pitch at our exact $39-ish price. Marketing
   on privacy ALONE = a premium option in a partly-free crowd with no separation.
2. **The moat is the COMBINATION.** Research found basically NO true all-in-one finance+health offline
   private apps. MRLN = finance + health + life tracking, offline, one app. That white space is nearly empty.
   That, not privacy by itself, is the defensible edge.

## POSITIONING SHIFT (recommendation)
Lead with the **combination**; privacy is the reason-to-believe underneath.
- Draft line: *"Your money and your health, in one place — and it never leaves your phone."*  [DRAFT—Akashi]
- The combo is the hook nobody can match; privacy is why they trust it.
- Supersedes the privacy-alone wedge in maki-first-10k-proof.md / maki-asset-drop-01.md — those hooks
  still work but should be reframed under the combo umbrella, not led by privacy solo.

## PRICING GUT-CHECK
At $6.99/mo / $59/yr, MRLN is at the TOP of the privacy-app range (competitors free–$1.99/mo, SenticMoney
$39/yr). Defensible ONLY if the combo + UI polish (Arthur's futuristic pass) visibly earn ~3× Finny.
Watch this — if conversion is weak at launch, price is the first suspect, combo-justification the second.
