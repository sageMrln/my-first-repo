## 2. The part nobody likes: you keep under half of every sale

Modeled from MRLN's own Danish tax engine (eff. ~40% at ~DKK 320k, rising to the ~55.9%
*skatteloft* ceiling) + public 2026 Danish/EU rates. **I'm a marketer, not your accountant —
confirm the structure with a real one. But the marketing implication holds regardless.**

On one **$40 key to an EU consumer:**
- 25% VAT (moms, EU B2C digital — usually baked into the price) → you're left with **$32**
- Stripe ~4% blended (small cross-border txns) → **~$30.40** to the business
- Danish personal income tax ~40% (low) to ~52% (top bracket) → **you net ~$15–$18**

So a "$40 product" puts **~$15–18 in your pocket.** That's not pessimism, it's the math, and it
changes strategy in three concrete ways:

1. **PRICE IS THE #1 LEVER YOU CONTROL.** Going $40 → $70 doesn't lose many buyers (privacy buyers
   aren't price-shopping a $30 delta) and nearly **halves** the number of sales every goal needs.
   At $70 you net ~$28–32/key → Goal 2 drops from ~300 to **~165 buyers/mo.** Same content, far
   less of it needed. **My recommendation: do not launch at $40. Test $59–79.**
2. **MARKET TOWARD LOWER-FRICTION GEOGRAPHIES.** EU B2C eats 25% VAT off the top. US/UK English
   audiences are bigger for our content anyway and the VAT picture differs for non-EU sales —
   confirm with the accountant, but the content language/targeting decision (lead in English,
   aim US/UK/global) is mine to make and it's the right call independent of tax.
3. **COMPANY vs PERSONAL.** An ApS (22% corporate) vs your personal top rate is thousands of
   kroner difference at Goal-2/3 volume. Your call + accountant's — but flag it *before* scale,
   not after.

## 3. The funnel math — sourced, not invented

Worked backwards from buyers, using live 2026 benchmarks (sources at bottom). This is the
**qualified-niche** path — we are NOT chasing raw virality, because $10k = a few hundred *right*
people, not millions of random views.

**Conversion stack (conservative / realistic / strong):**
- Views → profile/link tap (organic social): **0.5% / 0.75% / 1.0%** (organic is diluted; this is the honest cap)
- Landing → starts the free try: **20% / 25% / 30%** (warm — they clicked a privacy-app link on purpose)
- Free try → buys the key: **4% / 6% / 10%** (high-priced app median is 2.8%, top-quartile 6.1%; our buyer is higher-intent than cold app-store traffic, so 4–10% is defensible for motivated privacy users — this is the riskiest number and the one the first experiment must measure)

**Net landing-visitor → buyer:** ~0.8% / 1.5% / 3%.

**To hit Goal 1 (250 buyers at $40):**
| Scenario | Landing visitors needed | Cumulative views needed |
|----------|------------------------|--------------------------|
| Conservative | ~31,000 | **~6.2M views** |
| Realistic | ~16,500 | **~2.2M views** |
| Strong (niche, high-intent) | ~8,300 | **~0.8M views** |

At a **$70 price** (≈125 buyers), halve all of the above: realistic ≈ **~1.1M cumulative views.**

**What that actually means in calendar terms:** a new founder-led account posting 1–2x/day
typically runs 500–2,000 views/post for the first ~30 days while finding format, then climbs as
1–2 formats win and the occasional video breaks 50–200k. Reaching ~1–2M *cumulative* views is a
**~4–9 month grind**, not a stunt — and it hinges on two things I can't fake: landing 1–2
**repeatable winning formats**, and at least a couple of **breakout videos**. That's the realistic
timeline to the first $10k. Anyone who tells you 2 weeks is lying to you.
