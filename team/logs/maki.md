### 1) MARKET EXPECTATION MAP — table-stakes vs retention vs vanity
- **Table-stakes users EXPECT (and MRLN mostly has):** expense categories ✓, budget/limits ✓, income tracking ✓ (better: variable-income aware), recurring/subscription view ✓, savings goals ✓, debt/loan tracking ✓, net cash flow ✓, a "how am I doing" score ✓ (Stats & Grades), multi-currency/locale ✓.

So the market-expectation goal is NOT "match Monarch feature-for-feature." It's "be the best possible app for the privacy-motivated minority who will accept manual entry as the PRICE of never connecting a bank." Different segment, not a smaller Monarch.
