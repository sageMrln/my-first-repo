## (1) THE RULING — what "build this blueprint" means under S1/S2/S3
**The blueprint is adopted as MRLN's new default face, built as a phased system, published to customers ONCE.** Decided architecture:

1. **Skin and IA are separate axes, built skin-first in sequence but shipped together.** Tokens/typography land first because they are zero-visual-diff or test-decidable and cheaply revertible; the IA (5-item nav + sections) is a **new orthogonal `data-nav` axis in shared JS/DOM** (it does not exist today — 0 occurrences) with `switchTab`/`data-p` remaining the router primitive so all 8 deep-link sites, 221 i18n attrs and panel ids survive. It is **not** a 6th layout: the 5,944 B of layout rules restyle one flat DOM and cannot express hierarchy (canvas's own comment at :185–186 concedes 13 tabs won't fit abreast).
2. **Arthur's load-bearing sequencing call is adopted:** responsive nav shell with the **same 15 destinations** lands BEFORE the 5-section regroup, so "does the nav work on every device" and "is the map right" cannot hide each other's failures.
3. **One customer-visible ship.** Stages are independently gate-green and revertible commits on a redesign branch cut after RELEASE B closes; intermediate hybrid states never publish. If Osefe wants two visible releases as marketing beats, that is his call, priced at roughly double screenshot/i18n/gate churn (56 images + 8-language review + full 11-step pipeline, twice).
4. **The 5 existing layouts keep working untouched during the build** (base rules touched additively only). Their end-state is Osefe's decision (section 7); a permanent "Classic" dual design system is **forbidden** — the repo's own measured decay (74 colour literals surviving a sound token system, council ruling 2026-08-05 §0) proves this team cannot hold two systems conformant in a hand-maintained 2 MB file.
5. **S3 decided:** bottom bar ≤859px, **left sidebar with section sublists ≥860px** (reusing the app's own 859/860 boundary — measured: the only min-width media rules are 860 and the 1024 ambience gates; desktop is greenfield). A 320px audit is added (smallest existing rule: 330px).
