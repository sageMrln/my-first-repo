Our story

A small room, kept honest.

Café Nordlys started as a single espresso machine and a borrowed corner table. Today it's still small on purpose — a handful of tables, a counter worn smooth, and a kitchen that bakes before sunrise. Everything on the menu is simple, seasonal, and made to be eaten slowly.

We roast our house blend in small batches and change the bakes with the weather. Regulars know the corner seat by the window; first-timers usually find it too.

— The Nordlys kitchen
