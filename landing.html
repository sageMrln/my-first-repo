Good to know

Questions, answered plainly.

Why put everything in one app instead of five specialized ones?
Because your money, health and plans affect each other, and five apps can't see across themselves. In MRLN, a change to your budget updates your savings and your grade at once — and it's all under one private roof, on your device, with nothing syncing out.
Does MRLN connect to my bank?
No bank connection · MRLN records use this browser's storage and are not automatically sent to a MRLN backend.
Where is my data stored — the browser or the app?
Your data is stored locally on your device. The installed app may share storage with your browser or use separate storage, depending on the platform. If your data is not shown, import your backup. MRLN does not sync data between devices.
Can I move my data to a new phone?
Yes — you carry it over yourself. Use MRLN’s export control to create a file, then use its import control on the new device. Exported files leave browser storage only when you choose where to save or transfer them.
Is the assistant private?
Yes. The assistant runs on your device and answers from the numbers you've entered. Nothing is uploaded, and no outside AI service is called — asking it a question keeps your data exactly where it already was.
What happens if I stop paying, or MRLN disappears?
Entries save in this browser. Export a file when you want a backup or transfer. Exports are created only when you choose; where you store or share them is up to you.
Does it get slower as years of data build up?
MRLN works locally without waiting for a server. Performance still depends on your device, available storage and how much data you keep. Save regular backups as your history grows.
What if I lose my phone?
Straight answer: whatever's only on that phone is gone — same as any notes app. That's the honest trade for keeping nothing on a server. So once you've added a lot, MRLN nudges you to save a copy — back it up like you save your photos. It's a one-tap export; keep a copy somewhere safe and a new phone restores it in seconds.
Is it just for one person, or can a couple use it?
Either. There's no account tying MRLN to one identity, so a couple can each keep their own private file on their own phones, or share a single file on one device — whatever fits. Each access key unlocks the app; how you split things is up to you.
Is the Tax Helper official tax advice?
No. It gives an unofficial estimate across 12 countries to help you plan. It isn't tax advice — check with a professional for anything official.
Are the health features medical advice?
No. The gym, food and weight tools are for general information and tracking. They're not medical advice — talk to a professional about your health. MRLN is for ages 16 and up.
How much does it cost?
MRLN is a paid app — you buy an access key: $9.99/month, or $69/year (save 42%, under $6/month). There are no ads and your data is never sold, and it comes with a 30-day money-back guarantee.
Can I try it before I buy?
30-day money-back guarantee.
Why pay when there are free budgeting apps?
MRLN combines money, health, plans and notes in one offline app. Your subscription pays for access to these tools; there are no ads or tracking. Choose the tools that suit you best.
