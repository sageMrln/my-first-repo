Good to know

Questions, answered plainly.

Does MRLN connect to my bank?
No. MRLN never connects to a bank and never reads your transactions. You enter the numbers you want to track, and they stay on your phone.
Where is my data stored?
The numbers you enter are saved on your device. There's no account and no cloud copy. Loading the app itself uses a static web host, but what you type stays with you.
Does it work offline?
Yes. MRLN installs like an app and runs with no signal. The Assistant and every feature work fully offline.
Is the Tax Helper official tax advice?
No. It gives an unofficial estimate across 13 countries to help you plan. It isn't tax advice — check with a professional for anything official.
Are the health features medical advice?
No. The gym, food and weight tools are for general information and tracking. They're not medical advice — talk to a professional about your health. MRLN is for ages 16 and up.
How much does it cost?
MRLN is a paid app — you buy an access key: $6.99/month, or $59/year (save 30%, under $5/month). There are no ads and your data is never sold.
