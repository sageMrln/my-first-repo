Where your data lives

Your data stays on the device you use — and you can move it yourself.

Because nothing is stored on a server, your data lives in whatever you opened MRLN in. That's the whole point — and it means moving between devices is something you do, not something a cloud does for you.

🌐 In your browser

Open mrln.online in a browser and your data saves in that browser, on that device. Switch to a different browser or another phone and it starts empty — because your data never left the first one.

Saved in the app

Moving to a new phone or browser is a two-tap job you control: export a copy of your data to a file, open MRLN on the other device, and load it back in. Nothing syncs behind your back — and because there's no server, only you ever hold a copy. So keep one somewhere safe: if you clear your browser or lose the device, whatever was only there is gone. Even if your key lapses, you can always export your data straight from the lock screen — it's yours to keep.

Good to know

Questions, answered plainly.

Why put everything in one app instead of five specialized ones?
Because your money, health and plans affect each other, and five apps can't see across themselves. In MRLN, a change to your budget updates your savings and your grade at once — and it's all under one private roof, on your device, with nothing syncing out.
Does MRLN connect to my bank?
No — and there's no "connect your bank" button to hunt for. You type in what you want to track, and it never leaves your device.
Where is my data stored — the browser or the app?
Wherever you opened it. In a browser, your data saves in that browser on that device. Installed to your home screen, the app keeps its own storage on that device. Neither one is on a server, and neither syncs to the other — you move data between them yourself.
Can I move my data to a new phone?
Yes — you carry it over yourself. Export a copy of your data to a file, open MRLN on the new phone, and load it back in. It takes seconds, and no copy ever passes through a server on the way.
Is the assistant private?
Yes. The assistant runs on your device and answers from the numbers you've entered. Nothing is uploaded, and no outside AI service is called — asking it a question keeps your data exactly where it already was.
What happens if I stop paying, or MRLN disappears?
The app locks without an active key — but your data is never held hostage. You can export every number you've entered straight from the lock screen, as a plain file that's yours to keep. Nothing is deleted, and there's no server holding a copy to lose. Your data was always on your device; it stays there.
Does it get slower as years of data build up?
No. We loaded it with thousands of entries — the equivalent of five years of daily logging — and it still opened in well under a second and switched tabs without lag. Everything runs locally, so there's no network round-trip to wait on.
What if I lose my phone?
Straight answer: whatever's only on that phone is gone — same as any notes app. That's the honest trade for keeping nothing on a server. So once you've added a lot, MRLN nudges you to save a copy — back it up like you save your photos. It's a one-tap export; keep a copy somewhere safe and a new phone restores it in seconds.
Is it just for one person, or can a couple use it?
Either. There's no account tying MRLN to one identity, so a couple can each keep their own private file on their own phones, or share a single file on one device — whatever fits. Each access key unlocks the app; how you split things is up to you.
Is the Tax Helper official tax advice?
No. It gives an unofficial estimate across 13 countries to help you plan. It isn't tax advice — check with a professional for anything official.
Are the health features medical advice?
No. The gym, food and weight tools are for general information and tracking. They're not medical advice — talk to a professional about your health. MRLN is for ages 16 and up.
How much does it cost?
MRLN is a paid app — you buy an access key: $9.99/month, or $69/year (save 42%, under $6/month). There are no ads and your data is never sold, and it comes with a 30-day money-back guarantee.
